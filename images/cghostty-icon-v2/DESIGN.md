# cghostty 图标 v2

使用内置 imagegen 生成并整理的应用图标。白色陶瓷质感幽灵轮廓结合终端 `>_`，以薄荷绿色下划线呼应原生平滑光标；深蓝底色延续项目现有配色。没有文字标签，优先保证 Dock 小尺寸辨识度。

`artwork.png` 是最终完整方形画稿。`app-icon-1024.png` 由 Xcode 27 Icon Composer 的原生工具导出，包含 macOS 系统图标形状。应用使用 `../cghostty.icon`，旧 SVG 源文件保留。

生成提示词摘要：

> Create a centered, front-facing macOS terminal app icon: one minimal white satin ceramic ghost with a recessed deep navy chevron and a mint underscore cursor. Clean rounded silhouette, subtle top-left lighting, crisp edges, no text or extra decoration. Place it on a full-bleed deep navy gradient, with comfortable margins. Icon Composer supplies the outer macOS mask.

最终整理提示词：

> Keep the exact centered white ceramic ghost emblem and its navy > and mint underscore, preserving interior details and smooth silhouette. Replace all checkerboard and marks outside the ghost with a clean opaque deep navy background (#101D30), subtly lighter at top and darker at bottom, filling the entire square edge to edge. No rounded corners, border, transparent margin, checkerboard or new elements. Center the emblem with comfortable breathing room. Output square PNG.
